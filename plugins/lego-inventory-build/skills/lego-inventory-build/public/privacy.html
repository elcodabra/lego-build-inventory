Privacy policy

LEGO from my bricks (lego-mcp.vercel.app, lego-auth.vercel.app) is an app for ChatGPT and Claude that designs LEGO models from the parts you own and renders building instructions. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

What we store

  • Account. A passkey (public key only) and a random account id. No password, no e-mail, no name.
  • Your data. Your parts list and saved models, kept under your account so ChatGPT and Claude see the same data.
  • Media. Pictures and videos rendered for you. They are deleted after 30 days.
  • Photos of parts you upload are sent to Brickognize for recognition. Of a photo of a pile we keep only a cut-out of the parts themselves (background removed, about 1000 px) with your parts list, to open your build videos; it is replaced by your next pile photo and deleted when you clear the list. The original photo is not kept.
  • Usage analytics. Anonymous events (tool name, duration, status, counts) under a hashed id. Never tokens, names, e-mails, IP addresses, photos or part lists.

YouTube

Build videos made in the app (the animated instructions drawn from your parts, opening with the cut-out of your pile when there is one) are uploaded as unlisted videos to the app's own YouTube channel, so they can be shared and watched later. They contain no account data, names or faces. The videos are made mainly for children (a step-by-step build to follow with their own bricks), so they are marked "made for kids" on YouTube: no personalised ads and no comments under them. The app uses the YouTube Data API only for these uploads to its own channel; it does not access, read or change your YouTube account. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. To have a video removed, open an issue (link below).

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Data is processed by our hosting providers (Vercel, Upstash) only to run the service.

Your choices

Disconnect apps at lego-auth.vercel.app/account. Clear your parts list from the chat with the inventory_clear tool. To delete your account and all data, open an issue at github.com/elcodabra/lego-build-inventory.
