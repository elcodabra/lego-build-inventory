Privacy policy

LEGO from my bricks (lego-mcp.vercel.app, lego-auth.vercel.app) is an app for ChatGPT and Claude that designs LEGO models from the parts you own and renders building instructions. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

What we store

  • Account. A passkey (public key only) and a random account id. No password, no e-mail, no name.
  • Your data. Your parts list and saved models, kept under your account so ChatGPT and Claude see the same data.
  • Media. Pictures and videos rendered for you. They are deleted after 30 days.
  • Photos of parts you upload are sent to Brickognize for recognition and are not kept by us.
  • Usage analytics. Anonymous events (tool name, duration, status, counts) under a hashed id. Never tokens, names, e-mails, IP addresses, photos or part lists.

YouTube

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload build videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not access, read or change any user's YouTube account. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Data is processed by our hosting providers (Vercel, Upstash) only to run the service.

Your choices

Disconnect apps at lego-auth.vercel.app/account. Clear your parts list from the chat with the inventory_clear tool. To delete your account and all data, open an issue at github.com/elcodabra/lego-build-inventory.
